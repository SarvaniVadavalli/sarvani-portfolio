## 13. ScrollExpand Strategy

**Proposal:** Do not give all three projects the same full ScrollExpand treatment — the projects aren't equal in visual material or depth, and repeating the heaviest animation three times in a row will blunt its impact rather than build it.

- **UniMeet → Full ScrollExpand.** It's the most complete project (multiple roles, auth, real-time UI) and the one most likely to eventually have real UI visuals — the immersive treatment earns its place here.
- **Deepfake Image Detection → Full ScrollExpand.** Strongest technical/ML narrative (preprocessing → training → tuning) with a clear process to visualize step-by-step, which is exactly what ScrollExpand is good at.
- **Hospital Management System → Lighter treatment (static feature-card layout, not ScrollExpand).** It's schema/SQL-only right now with no described interface — giving it the same heavy scroll treatment as the other two would overstate its visual richness. A clean card-based presentation (schema highlights, stored procedures, CRUD) is more honest to what actually exists.

This keeps the two strongest, most complete projects as the visual centerpiece of the Projects section, while still giving the third full and fair coverage without inflating it.
